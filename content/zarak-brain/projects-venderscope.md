VenderScope is a continuous vendor risk intelligence platform built from the pain of managing 50+ vendor audits manually.

It monitors vendor risk signals across sources such as HIBP, NVD/NIST, Shodan, and Companies House, replacing annual point-in-time review with live weighted risk scoring and risk drift tracking.

It is designed to produce audit-ready PDF reports for ISO 27001 Annex A and Cyber Essentials-style reviews, written for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
