Maternify is a multilingual, safety-gated AI maternal advocacy platform, built as a team project at the VibeHack London 2026 hackathon — a production-ready MVP shipped in 24 hours, placing 5th in the Health Impact track.

It hardcodes a safety gate that bypasses AI generation entirely for clinical red-flags, routing users straight to 999/triage cards instead of risking an "AI reassurance" response during a medical emergency. Built on Next.js 14 with GLM-5.1 and Gemini (multimodal speech-to-text/OCR, embeddings for RAG, text-to-speech for midwife-script playback), with an in-memory RAG system over curated NHS and Tommy's documentation so every advocacy script is clinically evidenced and cited.

The goal: give minority-ethnic women an advocacy "voice" during pregnancy, addressing documented maternal mortality disparities (MBRRACE-UK) with clinically-grounded scripts rather than plain translation.
