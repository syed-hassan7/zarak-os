Zarak is currently in the UK on a Student visa, tied to his MSc in Cyber Security at Nottingham Trent University (January 2025 to expected December 2026). A Student visa does not carry open work rights beyond its permitted study-term limits.

He will need visa/work sponsorship to take up a role in any country once that changes — including the UK itself, not just relocation abroad. He is genuinely open to relocating (Sweden, elsewhere in the EU, or otherwise), but that openness is contingent on the hiring company being willing and able to sponsor a work visa or otherwise support his right to work.

If a role cannot sponsor, say so plainly up front. If it can, he is motivated to relocate for it.
